# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-08-21**

Fechtkarte does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. This policy is short because
there is not much to say.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything Fechtkarte stores stays on your device, in the app's own private storage:

- **The drill dataset** (109 historical cards) — bundled with the app, read-only, never
  transmitted anywhere.
- **Your preferences** — action count, thrust count, palette choices, and enabled generation
  rules — saved locally so they survive restarting the app.
- **Cards you save or share** — written to your device's own Pictures/Downloads folders (for
  Save PNG/PDF) or handed directly to another app you choose (for Share). Fechtkarte does not
  keep a copy of what you share, and does not upload anything to any server, because Fechtkarte
  has no server.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in, or registration of any kind.
- No network access — the app works fully offline and has no code path that sends data
  anywhere.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device data beyond
  what's needed to save or share a generated card, which only happens when you tap Save or
  Share.

## Third parties

Fechtkarte has no third-party integrations. It doesn't talk to any analytics, advertising, or
cloud service, so there is nothing to disclose about third-party data handling.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever needs to change — for example, if a future version adds a feature that
touches data in a new way — the change will be reflected here, dated, and described honestly in
the same terms as above.

## Contact

Fechtkarte is free and open-source software. Its complete source code is available for
inspection, so this policy's claims can be independently verified against the code that
actually ships.
